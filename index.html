Research & innovation

Data Context: a simpler way to run data platforms

EntrePro's research initiative, Data Context, extends SQL so that ingestion, transformation, governance, and machine learning can be expressed in a few readable lines instead of hundreds of lines of orchestration code. An ETL job that takes 139 lines across two languages in Airflow becomes 25 lines of SQL; a SageMaker training pipeline drops from 68 lines to 8.

The work is carried out with academic partners in the US and Europe and has been presented at the University of Louisville, NUIST in Nanjing, and Google Developer Group Sarajevo.

About

Dr. Farid Mehovic

Founder & Principal, EntrePro Corporation

Farid Mehovic has spent his career at the intersection of data, performance, and business value. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science at UCLA under Leonard Kleinrock, one of the fathers of the Internet, with a dissertation on the performance of database concurrency control, and has been building and leading data systems ever since.

He founded EntrePro Corporation in 1994 as a vehicle for consulting engagements that range from Fortune 500 companies to state government to venture-backed startups. In between, he has held senior data roles at IBM, Sabre, Facebook, Qubole, and SimplePractice, managing teams of up to 40 people across the US, Canada, India, and China. He has also taught database courses as an adjunct professor at San Diego State University and the University of Dallas.

EntrePro is a Texas corporation based in Austin, working with clients across the United States and internationally.
