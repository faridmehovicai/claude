  4. 2016Project · Patent

    Monitoring dormant accounts for resurrection

    Facebook (Meta) · with one teammate · sharded MySQL and DataStorm (Facebook's Airflow)

    Challenge

    An online advertising system manages accounts for a vast number of content providers and needs to re-engage the ones that have gone quiet, at the right moment and with the right message.

    Approach

    For each dormant account, the system checks whether a past campaign performed well against a benchmark such as a percentile of cost-per-objective for its country, page category, and objective type.   18. 1999Project

    Offline system for processing airline fares

    Sabre · Chief Architect · team of six within a program of 30

    Challenge

    A fares management system had to be built from scratch, driven by Y2K and the inflexibility of the legacy platform. Hundreds of hierarchical data structures represent airline pricing rules, and the new system had to integrate seamlessly with a limited clone of Sabre built for a client.

    Approach

    Three parts: normalized the hierarchical rule structures into a relational model; generated user-interface screens automatically from database metadata; and generated in-memory versions of the pricing data that the legacy clone could read. Chosen over two competing Sabre architectures, the automation produced 75% profitability on the roughly $10M subproject, which carried the entire $50M program.

  20. 1995Project · Patents

    Converting a legacy reservation system to relational

    Sabre · Lead Architect, Technology Planning and Architecture

    Challenge

    Demonstrate that reservation data could be modeled relationally and that the new and legacy systems could coexist during migration.

    Approach

    Built a prototype that compiles the unstructured passenger name record on the fly into SQL inserts on a separate Oracle server, virtually instantly, and converts an application's cryptic command syntax into SQL reads and writes against the same database. Both syntaxes follow a grammar, so Unix tools (lex and yacc) automated the conversion. The work produced two US patents.
