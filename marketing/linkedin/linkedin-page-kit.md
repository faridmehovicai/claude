# LinkedIn Company Page kit — EntrePro Corporation

Everything needed to create the page. Each field below is written to LinkedIn's
character limit, so it can be pasted as is.

## How to create the page (about 10 minutes)

1. Sign in to LinkedIn with your personal account, click the **For Business**
   grid icon (top right) → **Create a Company Page**.
2. Choose **Company**.
3. Fill in the fields from the "Page identity" section below. Upload
   `logo-300.png` as the logo.
4. Tick the verification box and click **Create page**.
5. On the new page, click **Edit page** and fill in the "About" and "Details"
   sections below. Upload `banner-1128x191.png` as the cover image.
6. Add the **Specialties** and **Hashtags**.
7. Publish the first post from the "Launch posts" section, then share it from
   your personal profile and invite your connections to follow the page.

## About (2,000 characters max; this is about 1,650)

EntrePro Corporation helps executives build the data function their company actually needs: a clear strategy, a sound architecture, and systems that perform.

Founded in 1994 and led by Dr. Farid Mehovic, EntrePro works across the full span of a company's data — from the application database to the executive dashboard. Engagements range from Fortune 500 companies to state government to venture-backed SaaS startups, and every one is led personally by Dr. Mehovic, hands-on, with no hand-offs to junior staff.

What we do:
• Fractional or interim Chief Data Officer
• Enterprise data strategy spanning architecture, engineering, analytics, data science & AI, and governance
• Enterprise data architecture, data warehousing, and data engineering
• Database design and order-of-magnitude performance improvement
• Data migration, integration, and master data management
• Executive analytics, BI, and KPI programs
• Data governance, quality, and protection
• Technical due diligence and rescue engagements

Selected results: a 19-node massively parallel warehouse delivered at roughly a quarter of a peer agency's cost, saving an estimated $22M; 75% margin on a $10M airline fares system; 10× performance on a 3-billion-row database; record matching three times more accurate than the leading commercial tool.

Dr. Mehovic earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science at UCLA under Leonard Kleinrock, holds three US patents in data management, and has held senior data roles at IBM, Sabre, Facebook, Qubole, and SimplePractice.

Based in La Jolla, California. Working with clients across the United States and internationally.

### Post 4 — fractional CDO

Startups think about data leadership last, or not at all.

That's understandable: the chief data officer role has never been well defined, and it comes in many flavors. But data spans the whole company from day one, and the decisions made early about models, databases, and ownership are expensive to unwind later.

A fractional CDO gives a growing company that seat at the table for one to three days a week, without a full-time hire. I've done it for SimplePractice through its IPO, for Druva, for Electric Cab North America, and for Overjet.

What it covers: https://entrepro.biz/services.html
